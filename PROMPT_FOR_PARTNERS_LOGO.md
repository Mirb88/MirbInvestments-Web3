# Prompt for MirbInvestments Partner Logo Generation (for Microsoft Copilot / DALL-E 3)

## Core Request:

Generate a high-end, abstract, 3D glass icon for the "Trusted Partners" section of a modern crypto platform. The icon must be visually striking, professional, and symbolize partnership, technology, and trust.

---

### **Prompt (Copy and Paste this):**

**A single, centered, photorealistic 3D icon of two interlocking, flowing shapes made of polished, transparent glass. The icon floats against a stark, pitch-black background (`#0D0D0D`). The edges of the glass shapes should catch and refract a subtle, internal glow of vibrant Neon Green (`#2FE93D`). The composition is minimalist, ultra-clean, and has a high-tech, premium feel. The icon is perfectly circular, with dramatic studio lighting that creates soft highlights and deep, clean shadows on the glass surface. No other elements, no text, 1:1 aspect ratio.**

---

## Instructions for Variations (for Different Partners):

The prompt above uses the primary **Neon Green** color. To create unique icons for each partner (OKX, Bitget, Binance, etc.), simply change the color name in the prompt.

**Example for a Partner with a Blue Theme (like OKX):**

"A single, centered, photorealistic 3D icon of two interlocking, flowing shapes made of polished, transparent glass. The icon floats against a stark, pitch-black background (`#0D0D0D`). The edges of the glass shapes should catch and refract a subtle, internal glow of vibrant **Electric Blue**. The composition is minimalist, ultra-clean, and has a high-tech, premium feel. The icon is perfectly circular, with dramatic studio lighting that creates soft highlights and deep, clean shadows on the glass surface. No other elements, no text, 1:1 aspect ratio."

**Suggested Colors to Try:**
*   For Binance: **BNB Gold** or **Vibrant Yellow**
*   For Bitget: **Royal Blue**
*   For OKX: **Electric Blue** or **Cyan**
*   For a neutral/new partner: **Platinum Silver** or **Finance White**

## Key Elements in the Prompt:
-   **Photorealistic 3D icon:** Ensures high quality and depth.
-   **Polished, transparent glass:** Gives a modern, premium, and clean aesthetic.
-   **Interlocking, flowing shapes:** Symbolizes partnership and collaboration.
-   **Pitch-black background:** Matches your app's theme and makes the icon stand out.
-   **Internal glow of [COLOR]:** This is the part you change to customize for each partner, providing brand alignment while maintaining a consistent style.
-   **Minimalist, ultra-clean:** Prevents the AI from adding unnecessary clutter.
-   **Perfectly circular:** Ensures it fits perfectly in the designated space.
-   **1:1 aspect ratio:** Guarantees a square image, ideal for circular masking.
