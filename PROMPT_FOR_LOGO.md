# Prompt for MirbInvestments Logo Generation (for ChatGPT/DALL-E)

## Core Request:

Generate a high-end, minimalist vector logo for a modern crypto investment platform named "MirbInvestments". The logo must be instantly recognizable, scalable, and embody the core brand values of simplicity, high technology, security, and financial growth. It should be suitable for a dark-themed application.

---

### **Prompt Version 1: Abstract & Minimalist**

**Full Prompt:**
"Create a minimalist, flat 2D vector logo for 'MirbInvestments'. The design should be an abstract, geometric representation of the letter 'M'. The lines of the 'M' should subtly transform into an upward-trending financial chart or a growth arrow. The logo must be composed of clean, sharp lines with a professional and high-tech feel.

**Color Palette:** Use only the following colors: the main symbol should be in a vibrant Neon Green (`#2FE93D`), with a secondary accent of BNB Gold (`#F0B90B`). The background must be Onyx Black (`#0D0D0D`).

**Style:** The logo should be extremely clean, suitable for a modern fintech company. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects. The output must be a single, iconic symbol on a plain background."

**Keywords:** `minimalist, vector, abstract, geometric, fintech, growth, clean lines, 2D, flat design`

---

### **Prompt Version 2: Shield & Growth Concept**

**Full Prompt:**
"Design a modern logo for 'MirbInvestments' that combines the concepts of security and growth. The logo should feature a stylized, minimalist shield. Inside the shield, integrate an upward-pointing arrow or a simple, rising line graph. The design should be clean, iconic, and communicate trust and technological sophistication.

**Color Palette:** The shield outline and the growth element should be primarily in Neon Green (`#2FE93D`). Use a small, subtle accent of BNB Gold (`#F0B90B`) within the design. The background should be a dark Onyx Black (`#0D0D0D`).

**Style:** Flat, 2D vector logo. Avoid any complex details or illustrations. The final result should be a strong, simple mark that works well at all sizes. No text."

**Keywords:** `shield, security, growth arrow, modern, vector, minimalist, trust, fintech, iconic`

One of the strokes of the 'M' should extend upwards, forming a clear growth indicator. The overall feeling should be dynamic, intelligent, and cutting-edge.

**Color Palette:** The primary lines of the 'M' should be in Neon Green (`#2FE93D`). The background must be Onyx Black (`#0D0D0D`). No other colors.

## **Important Instructions for All Prompts:**

-   **Output Format:** Provide the logo as a high-resolution image with a solid background.
-   **No Text:** Do not include the name "MirbInvestments" in the logo itself. The symbol must stand alone.
-   **Focus on Simplicity:** The final design should be reducible to a simple icon, suitable for use as a favicon or app icon.
-   **Avoid:** Do not use literal representations of coins, dollar signs, or complex chart graphics. The design must be abstract and modern.
